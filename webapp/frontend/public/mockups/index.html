Three more, this time moving.

v4–v6 put the generation mix in the center and let it move. Open one and let it run for 10 seconds before judging — the magic is in the motion. Still want the static set?

v4 · Particle Flow

DYNAMIC

Energy is a river. Each fuel emits a stream of colored particles from the left column toward the load on the right. Coal flows slow & dark, wind gusts in waves, solar bursts in pulses, batteries spike when they dispatch. You can see which fuel is doing the work right now.

canvas particlesvibe-coded fuel character~1200 particles @ 60fps

v5 · Living Stack

DYNAMIC

The grid as an aquarium. One big region column showing fuels stacked floor-to-ceiling, each band's surface rippling with its own wave physics (coal is calm, solar choppy, battery wild). Dashed "ghost" lines mark where each layer was 24 h ago. Bubbles rise for every dispatched unit.

SVG wave physics24h ghost overlaylive bubble stream

v6 · Pulse Map

DYNAMIC

Australia as a night sky. Every generator is a star, sized & glowing by current MW; same-fuel stars are linked into faint constellations. Interconnectors are flowing light streams whose direction shows which way power is moving. Cities are warm halos of demand. 24-h playback strip lets you watch the constellations breathe.

SVG starfield · 200+ nodesanimated flow lines24h playback
When to pick v4

You want the homepage to instantly answer "where is the energy coming from right now?" The particles literally show fuel → region flow — like watching a power grid breathe.

When to pick v5

You like time-comparisons. The big aquarium with its 24h-ago ghost lines is the most readable answer to "are renewables actually displacing coal today?" Each fuel even has its own wave character.

When to pick v6

You want a screenshot people will share. Geographic context (which states, which interconnectors) plus the night-sky aesthetic. Best demo material, also genuinely useful for spotting regional concentration.
